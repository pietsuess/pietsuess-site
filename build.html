Dear Deer Website

A joyous home for Dear Deer Improv, performing monthly shows since 2022, built to play like one of their shows: it takes a suggestion and runs with it. A cartoon glove stands in for the mouse and has an opinion about everything: a thumbs up after a click, the horns for the cast, a wagging finger at the podcast sign that still says Coming Soon. Click the deer in the menu and it runs off into Shows, knocking the menu words into each other until the Instagram icon bounces off the edge of the window. The buck charges the wall and doubles back through the letters, scattering them as it goes. The music man rolls along a piano keyboard playing jazz, and you can play the keys too. The corporate badge swings on its lanyard when you grab it. One cast bio changes voice every time it opens: mutinous pirate, condescending alien, long lost twin. Even the contact form asks for a suggestion. An irreverent and playful rendition of a standard homepage.

Documentary Film Website

Thoughtful Robot Productions · INTUITION
A home for INTUITION, a feature documentary exploring the miraculous world of children's intuition, on a journey from New York City to Bangalore to Sydney. The title fades up through a soft cloud, its letters stirred by invisible hands, and once it settles your mouse stirs them too. From there the whole page is one scroll-driven sequence: quotes come in over loops from the film, and on a phone the frame follows a girl as she walks across a hillside. Dots down the side jump to each part. The team opens in its own window, one bio after the next. Sign-ups from the site and its QR page for screenings land in the film's own mailing list on Cloudflare, with a private page where the team looks up and edits every contact.
